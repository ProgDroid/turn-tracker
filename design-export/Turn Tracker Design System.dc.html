A no-install, pass-the-phone turn coordinator for in-person board games. Dark & focused direction: a calm near-black UI that stays out of the way, with one vivid emerald that owns the moment it’s your turn. Mobile-first, built for phones held at a dim table.

01 / Direction

The hero moment, three ways

The full-screen “your turn” state is the emotional core. I sketched it in three directions so we’re choosing the feeling, not just a color. Committing to A — Dark & focused: it reads instantly across a table, is kind to eyes in a dim room, and lets the emerald flood do the shouting.

WHY A WINS
  • The emerald flood is unmissable in peripheral vision — you don’t need to be reading the screen.
  • Calm near-black everywhere else means the turn moment is the only loud thing in the app.
  • Low light output by default; the room stays dark and relaxed between turns.
  • One accent = one mental model. Emerald always and only means “go.”

05 / Behaviour

Motion & accessibility

MOTION NOTES
Turn change → hero

On becoming current: emerald floods in from the Done button’s position (radial wipe, 560ms ease-standard); “IT’S YOUR TURN” rises 12px and fades in; the emblem starts a 2.4s pulse loop. Fires navigator.vibrate([0,80,40,80]) + a short chime, and acquires the Screen Wake Lock.

Nudge feedback

Sender: button does a quick scale 0.96→1 spring. Recipient: toast drops in and shakes once (600ms), emblem pulse speeds to 1.6s for 3s, double-buzz.

prefers-reduced-motion

No wipe, pulse, shake, or sheen. The hero state cross-fades (120ms opacity only), the emblem is static, and the nudge toast appears without shaking. Vibration + sound still fire — they’re not motion.

ACCESSIBILITY NOTES
AA
Contrast

Body text #F4F4F5 on #0A0A0C ≈ 18:1; ink #04140D on emerald ≈ 12.8:1. Muted text holds ≥4.5:1. All pass WCAG AA, most AAA.

☉
Touch targets

Min 56px tall (well over the 44px floor). The Done button is ~150px and fills the thumb zone — no aiming required.

◉
Never color alone

Connection, host, skip and turn states all pair color with a text label or icon. Status dots always sit beside words; the “your turn” state is carried by full-screen layout + text + buzz, not hue.

⚡
Non-visual cues

Becoming active triggers vibration + sound, so a pocketed or glanced-away phone still signals. aria-live announces “It’s your turn” and nudges for screen readers.
